ORIGIN

WHY WE WERE ESTABLISHED

For years, families in Pirpai, Pabbi, and across District Nowshera had to travel arduous distances to major provincial hubs for critical maternal deliveries, high-risk pregnancy monitoring, and fertility procedures. Dr. Sadaf Sarwar established this center to root world-class clinical standards right here on Main GT Road.

  • Eliminating long-distance travel distress for expectant mothers
  • Comprehensive diagnostics and pharmacy immediately accessible
  • Affordable, compassionate, community-focused healthcare
CLINICAL EXCELLENCE

WHAT MAKES US DIFFERENT

Every consultation is handled with clinical depth, extreme gentleness, and strict ethical standards. We unite 19+ years of senior governmental and private surgical mastery with modern ultrasound diagnostics, sterility-controlled minor and major procedures, and empathetic patient counseling.

  • Direct oversight by District Gynecologist Dr. Sadaf Sarwar
  • Tailored fertility protocols grounded in modern evidence
  • Private, respectful environment preserving every patient's dignity

FOUNDER & DISTRICT GYNECOLOGIST

Dr. Sadaf Sarwar

Obstetrician, Gynecologist & Infertility Specialist

MBBS DGO ACRM (Infertility) Cosmetic Gynecology Member, SOGP

Dr. Sadaf Sarwar serves as District Gynecologist at Mian Rashid Hussain Shaheed Hospital, Pabbi Nowshera, with more than 19 years of dedicated clinical practice in obstetrics and gynecology.

She earned her MBBS from the esteemed Fatima Jinnah Medical College, Lahore (2005), and completed her postgraduate Diploma in Gynecology & Obstetrics (DGO) from Lady Reading Hospital, Peshawar.

Her clinical expertise spans normal and instrumental deliveries, cesarean sections, D&C, laparotomy, hysterectomy, and complex vaginal wall repairs, alongside specialized infertility management and advanced cosmetic gynecology.
